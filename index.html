ML PLATFORM & ORCHESTRATION

WFT AI Twin

Problem statement

Physical wheel-force testing requires dedicated vehicle test runs. WFT AI Twin provides a virtual alternative: engineers upload vehicle telemetry CSVs, and machine-learning models predict six wheel-load components (Fx, Fy, Fz, Mx, My, Mz) for each wheel. The platform also supports retraining as new labelled data becomes available.

My contribution

Developed backend APIs, database access and asynchronous processing workflows. Owned retraining orchestration and contributed to model lifecycle management and results dashboards. My focus was the platform and orchestration; the modeling team developed the predictive models.
